The problem

Sponsoring a conference is expensive. Not having a plan is more expensive.

Booth space, travel, swag, sponsorship fees, staff time, and demo prep add up fast. And yet most exhibitors show up with no real strategy for turning booth traffic into pipeline, no plan for the hallway conversations that actually move deals, and no follow-up system once the show ends.

The result: a lot of spend, a stack of scanned badges, and very little to show for it. That's the gap 10X Conference Coaching closes.

The approach

A plan for before, during, and after your event

01 Before

Walk in with a plan, not just a booth

Define your goals, sharpen your pitch, prep your team, and build a pre-event outreach plan so the right people already know to find you.

02 During

Turn foot traffic into real conversations

Booth engagement tactics, presentation and demo coaching, and networking strategy that gets you in front of the people who actually buy.

03 After

Make the follow-up actually happen

A structured follow-up system so leads don't go cold the week you get back to the office, turning contacts into pipeline.
